# Pandora Community Code of Conduct

Pandora's community should be safe, practical, and welcoming. This applies to
issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, project events, and public spaces
where someone is representing Pandora.

## Expected conduct

- Treat people with respect, including when you disagree with their design or
  code.
- Critique the work with specific evidence. Do not attack the person.
- Give credit for ideas, code, research, and assets.
- Respect privacy, stated boundaries, and requests to stop an interaction.
- Accept correction, repair harm where possible, and help newcomers understand
  the project's rules.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, personal attacks,
doxxing, deliberate intimidation, impersonation, plagiarism, and repeated
disruption are not accepted. Publishing private reports, credentials, prompts,
or personal information is also prohibited.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not post sensitive incident details in a public issue. Contact a maintainer
privately through an available GitHub profile contact. If no private project
channel is available, open a minimal issue asking for one without naming people
or describing the incident, or use GitHub's Report Abuse process when immediate
safety is involved.

Maintainers will limit access to a report, hear the people involved, document
the decision, and protect the reporter's privacy as far as the platform allows.
Responses may include a private warning, limits on participation, a temporary
suspension, or a permanent ban. Serious incidents may skip earlier steps.
Maintainers must follow the same rules and must step away from decisions where
they have a conflict of interest.
